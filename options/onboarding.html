Help improve OTNow?

OTNow works fully without sharing statistics. Nothing is shared unless you choose to enable it. If you opt in, it sends a small anonymous numerical report about once a day, and you can turn this off later in Settings.

Shared only if you opt in

A random installation ID, the OTNow version, and numerical totals for item types, reminders, moved deadlines, manual check-offs, successful refreshes, and days used.

What stays local

Names, courses, coursework titles, URLs, due dates, grades, emails, student numbers, Canvas IDs, passwords, and authentication information.

Example report: 18 assignments · 4 quizzes · 3 reminders · 1 moved date
