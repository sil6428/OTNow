Help improve OTNow?

You can optionally share anonymous numerical totals that help measure whether OTNow is useful. Nothing is shared unless you choose to enable it.

What would be shared

A random installation ID, the OTNow version, and totals for broad item types, reminders, moved deadlines, manual check-offs, successful refreshes, and days used.

What is never shared

Names, course names or codes, coursework titles, URLs, due dates, grades, emails, student numbers, Canvas IDs, passwords, or authentication information.

If enabled, OTNow reports about once a day. You can turn it off later in Settings, which deletes this installation's aggregate row.

Your anonymous signal is live.
